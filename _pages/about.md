Hi there, welcome to my website!

I am a master's student at the Paris School of Economics (PSE) in the Analysis and Policy in Economics (APE) programme, jointly offered with EHESS, ENS–PSL, l'École nationale des ponts et chaussées, and Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne University. I am also a Research Assistant at the University of Copenhagen (UCPH), working with Jakob Roland Munch, Chairman of the Danish Economic Councils. Previously, I was a Research Consultant at the European Central Bank (ECB) and a Research Assistant at the Center for Economic Behavior and Inequality (CEBI).
